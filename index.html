Thinking Modes

Multiple ways to sharpen your thinking.

Choose how you want Thinkerton to reason through your problem.

◆

"Think Like" Mode

Describe a decision and see it analyzed through the specific lens of a chosen thinker — Singleton's capital allocation logic, Munger's inversion thinking, Kahneman's bias-correction framework. Compare perspectives side by side.

◇

Decision Journal

Log your decisions over time. Thinkerton maps each one against relevant mental models, flags cognitive biases, and shows where your reasoning diverges from how elite thinkers would approach the same problem.

◊

Reasoning Chains

Not just "what would Buffett say" — structured outputs showing the mental model being applied, the chain of logic, the historical precedent, and the conclusion. Full transparency into the reasoning.

○

Adversarial Review

Submit your own analysis and let Thinkerton poke holes in it using the frameworks of history's most rigorous thinkers. Find the blind spots before they become costly mistakes.
